# Balance analysis: feature definitions

One row = one FIT record (about 1 s). Rows from all validated rides are pooled.
Generated by `scripts/balance_features.py` into `output/balance_features.parquet` (git-ignored).

## Conventions

- Every feature is read directly from the FIT file or the Garmin activity index, or is simple arithmetic on those values (unit conversion, mean, time window, ratio). No inference, no modelling, no guessing.
- Missing stays missing. Stored as null. Never filled with 0, a neighbouring value or an estimate.
- Metric units, or the standard unit for the quantity. Every column states its unit.
- Values are as decoded by `garmin-fit-sdk` (scale and offset already applied).
- Time in seconds (s), counted from the first record of the ride.
- Windows are trailing and time-based: the window at time t covers samples with time in (t − window, t].
- A trailing mean is empty until the ride has run for the full window, so no partial windows at the start of a ride.
- A usable sample has a known side (`left_pct` not empty) and power > 0.
- Rides: the 140 rides whose balance validates against Garmin's `avgLeftBalance` (`output/balance_rides.csv`, status `ok`).

## Identifiers

| Column | Definition | Unit | Source and notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `activity_id` | Garmin activity ID | integer | Activity index. Groups rows by ride, for ride-level error clustering. |

## Target

| Column | Definition | Unit | Source and notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `left_pct` | Left leg share of power, decoded from `left_right_balance` | % | FIT field. Empty when the side is unclear: no right flag, raw `0x80` (`'right'`), or value over 100%. Also empty when left is 5% or less, or 95% or more (data-quality band, see note below). |
| `left_pct_10s` | Trailing 10 s mean of `left_pct` over usable samples | % | Mean. Empty if fewer than 5 usable samples in the window. |
| `left_sd_60s` | Trailing 60 s population SD of `left_pct` over usable samples (variability outcome) | % (percentage points) | Empty if fewer than 30 usable samples in the window, or the ride has run less than 60 s. Declared before the first model run, see `docs/balance_analysis.md`. |

Note on the data-quality band: samples with left at 5% or less, or 95% or more, are excluded. This is a data-quality decision, not a FIT rule. The 0% spike (about 1,345 samples at exactly 0%) falls inside this band.

## Drivers

| Column | Definition | Unit | Source and notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `power` | Record `power` | W | FIT field. Zero kept in the table, excluded from the target analysis. |
| `power_60s` | Trailing 60 s mean of `power`, zeros included | W | Mean. |
| `cadence` | Record `cadence` | rpm | FIT field, integer. `fractional_cadence` not used. |
| `crank_torque` | `power ÷ (cadence × 2π / 60)` | N·m | Arithmetic. Empty when cadence < 30 rpm, to avoid dividing by near-zero angular speed. |
| `heart_rate` | Record `heart_rate` | bpm | FIT field. |
| `heart_rate_30s` | Trailing 30 s mean of `heart_rate` | bpm | Mean. Only heart-rate window used. |

## Time and fatigue

| Column | Definition | Unit | Source and notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `elapsed_s` | Seconds since the first record of the ride | s | Timestamp difference. |
| `ride_fraction` | `elapsed_s ÷ elapsed_s of the last record` | dimensionless, 0–1 | Uses the records, not the session `total_elapsed_time`, which can be shorter than the records. |
| `cum_work_kJ` | Cumulative work: `accumulated_power ÷ 1000` | kJ | FIT field. The profile labels `accumulated_power` as "watts", but its last value equals the session `total_work` in J, so it is cumulative energy. |
| `since_break_s` | Seconds since the end of the last pause. Counts from ride start if no pause has ended yet. | s | Pauses come directly from the FIT `timer` events: a `stop_all` followed by the next `start`. Only pauses of at least 5 minutes (300 s) count, auto or manual. Shorter pauses are ignored. A `stop_all` with no following `start` (the end of the ride) is not a pause. Only past events are used. |
| `hr_power_drift` | `(trailing 5 min mean HR ÷ trailing 5 min mean power) ÷ (same ratio over the first 20 min) − 1` | % | Ratio of means. Empty for rides shorter than 20 min (session `total_elapsed_time` < 1200 s), before the first 20 min have passed (baseline not complete, so no later data is used), when baseline power is zero, or when trailing 5 min power is below 50% of the baseline mean power (coasting, stops). Positive = heart rate rising relative to power. |

## Context

| Column | Definition | Unit | Source and notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `indoor` | 1 if activity type is `indoor_cycling`, else 0 | boolean (0/1) | Activity type from the Garmin activity index. Always included as a control. |
| `gradient_15s` | Altitude change ÷ distance covered over the trailing 15 s, × 100. Reference point: latest record at or before t − 15 s. | % | Arithmetic on FIT `enhanced_altitude` and `distance`. Empty for indoor rides, without a reference point, or when less than 30 m was covered in the window (below that, altitude noise dominates). |
| `speed` | Record `enhanced_speed` × 3.6 | km/h | FIT field in m/s, unit conversion only. |
| `year` | Calendar year of the ride start | year (integer) | From the activity index. |
| `temperature` | Record `temperature` | °C | FIT field, integer. Device reading, not checked against air temperature. |
| `respiration_rate` | Record `enhanced_respiration_rate` | breaths/min | FIT field. Device estimate, not a direct measurement. Reported for almost all records. |

## Why these rules

Found in the first extraction. Each one was a definition artifact, not a decoding error:

- **`gradient_15s` needs 30 m:** with little distance covered, altitude wobble dominates. Before: 2,064 rows above |30%|, minimum about −11,800%.
- **`hr_power_drift` needs 50% of baseline power:** a ratio of means explodes when power is near zero. Before: 65,241 rows above 100%, maximum about 598,500%. A relative cut is the same for every rider.
- **`ride_fraction` uses the record span:** the session total was 231 s shorter than the records in the worst ride, so the value reached 1.016.

## Mechanism only (not predictors)

These restate the same forces that produce balance, from the same meter. Use them to explain a pattern, not to predict it. Screened in `balance_screen.py` and `balance_partial.py`.

| Column | Definition | Unit | Source and notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `left_torque_eff`, `right_torque_eff` | Record `left/right_torque_effectiveness` | % | FIT field. 0 is treated as missing (no reading, about 3% of records). Reported for 32 road rides, all in 2026. |
| `left_pedal_smooth`, `right_pedal_smooth` | Record `left/right_pedal_smoothness` | % | FIT field. 0 is treated as missing. Same 32 road rides as torque effectiveness. |
| `left_pco`, `right_pco` | Record `left/right_pco` | mm | FIT field, integer. 0 is a valid value (centred). Reported for all 87 road rides. |
| `left_phase_deg`, `right_phase_deg` | Power phase span: end angle minus start angle of `left/right_power_phase`, mod 360 | ° | FIT gives a [start, end] pair in degrees. The span is how far through the crank cycle the leg applies power. Empty where not reported. Reported for all 87 road rides. |

## Ride-level covariates (not per sample)

One value per road ride, from the FIT session message. Used in `balance_rides_level.py`: Spearman against the ride's mean left %, Holm across covariates.

| Field | Unit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| average power, normalised power | W | |
| intensity factor, training stress score | dimensionless | |
| average heart rate, average cadence | bpm, rpm | |
| total ascent, total elapsed time, total work | m, s, kJ | |
| average temperature | °C | |
| workout RPE, workout feel | scale | User-entered. Only 60 rides have a value. |

Date (years since 1 Jan 2024, from the activity start) is tested in the same script, plus a version with ride mean power held fixed.

Not used: time standing and stand count. Session values exist for 8 rides only, and the stand count does not add up (see "Not used: standing").

## Not used: standing

The FIT `rider_position_change` events (`seated`, `standing`, `transition_to_standing`, `transition_to_seated`) are present for all 87 kept road rides and for 24 of 53 kept indoor rides. They do not add up, so they are not used:

- **Sequence check:** no road ride follows a consistent seated/standing sequence. Transitions are often followed by `seated`, `standing` appears directly after `seated`, and events repeat.
- **Count check:** the number of `transition_to_standing` events equals the session `stand_count` in 4 of 87 road rides.

Rule: a standing source is used only if it adds up. If it does not, it is ignored. The check above was not designed to pass any ride; it reports the consistency of the event sequence as recorded.
